We are currently trading at $354.11, which is dangerously close to the Bollinger Lower Band at $346.81. When a stock drifts toward the lower band during a period of bearish momentum, the risk of a volatility expansion to the downside is significantly higher than the probability of an immediate, smooth breakout to the upside. 

The aggressive analyst calls the current neutral RSI a "gift." I call it a warning. An RSI of 45.62 confirms that there is absolutely no buying conviction in the market right now. Betting against that lack of conviction is not investing; it is gambling on a reversal that the technicals simply do not support. Furthermore, the "wait-and-see" approach is not about missing an opportunity; it is about capital preservation. If the price breaches the $346.57 support level—a distinct possibility given the negative MACD—we will be forced to cut the position at a loss, potentially during a period of high slippage. 

True sustainability comes from waiting for the trend to define itself. If the price recovers above the 10-day EMA, we will have a confirmed signal that the bulls are back in control, and we can enter with a much higher probability of success. Until then, my recommendation remains steadfast: maintain a neutral posture, preserve our liquidity, and refuse to gamble on a "squeezed" trade that currently offers more downside risk than verifiable upside potential. Protecting our assets means avoiding the "shakeout" entirely, not hoping we aren't the ones being shaken out.
