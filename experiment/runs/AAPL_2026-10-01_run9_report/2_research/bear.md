When you rely on high-margin services to bolster earnings, you are inherently dependent on the installed base of hardware users. If innovation plateaus and upgrade cycles lengthen, that "recurring revenue" engine loses its fuel. The market is currently pricing in this uncertainty, which is exactly why the RSI is flat and the MACD has turned negative.
