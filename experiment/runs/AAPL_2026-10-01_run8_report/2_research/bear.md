You point to the 50 SMA (321.86) as a "reliable floor." Respectfully, that is a dangerous assumption. ### Competitive Stagnation and Market Saturation
Your argument about the "unparalleled ecosystem" misses a fundamental shift in the tech landscape. **AAPL** is the victim of its own success. When you have achieved peak saturation in high-margin markets, growth cannot be sustained by hardware cycles alone. 

We are seeing a trend where consumer upgrade cycles for flagship devices are lengthening. While you see "hardware-software synergy," I see a company struggling to justify premium pricing in an era where mid-range competitors are offering 90% of the functionality at 50% of the cost. The "services" growth you rely on is tethered to the health of the installed base—if that base stops expanding because the hardware barrier is too high, the services engine eventually stalls.

You claim this is where "weak hands are shaken out." That’s a narrative, not a risk-management strategy.
