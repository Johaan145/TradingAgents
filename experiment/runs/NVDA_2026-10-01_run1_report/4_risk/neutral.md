If the stock pulls back toward the 10-day EMA at $226.43, we use the remaining half of our allocation to scale in. Let's widen the stop slightly to account for the 5.82 ATR—perhaps shifting it closer to $215.00—while keeping our total position size within that original 1-2% cap. 

This approach is balanced. It respects the momentum that the Aggressive Analyst is banking on, while acknowledging the volatility and resistance levels that worry the Conservative Analyst. We aren't chasing the top, and we aren't waiting for a miracle dip. We are entering with a plan that allows us to manage risk as the trade develops.
